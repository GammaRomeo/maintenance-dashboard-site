Before you start

Torque values and specs elsewhere in this app are reference information only, compiled from a mix of official service literature, community/tuner sources, and — where marked — self-reported by other users and not independently verified.

Always verify safety-critical specs — wheels, suspension, brakes, steering — against OEM documentation before doing any work. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any vehicle manufacturer.

Use of this app and its data is at your own risk.
